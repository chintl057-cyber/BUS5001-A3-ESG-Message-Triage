containing exactly the eight required fields.

- WASTE_MANAGEMENT

- SUPPLIER_COMPLIANCE

POSITIVE, NEUTRAL, or NEGATIVE.

Y if the issue needs investigation or action; otherwise N.

- LOW: No identifiable personal or confidential information.
